About

Hi, I'm Gaia.

I'm a licensed mental health counselor, and I've been rooted in the Bloomington community for years. I help individuals and families through relationship stress, grief and loss, school concerns, family conflict, trauma and toxic stress, depression, anxiety and ADHD.

I come from a non-traditional family myself, and I welcome all people and family systems. Some of our work may look back at the patterns we learned growing up. Some of it is about how your family is working right now, day to day.

My approach is person-centered. I draw on evidence-based practices like Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Collaborative Problem Solving and Motivational Interviewing, and I fit them to you rather than the other way around.
